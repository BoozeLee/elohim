# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists because plausible-looking wrong numbers are hard to catch.
The behaviour we want is the behaviour the project is about: check the
arithmetic, write down what you actually measured, and say so when it
contradicts you.

## Expected

- Argue about the number, not the person. "Your expectation is wrong and here
  is the residual" is the ideal comment.
- Report a refutation as a result, not a defeat. A measured contradiction is
  the most valuable thing anyone can add here.
- Say which of your claims are measured, derived, or guessed.
- Take a correction in public gracefully. Everyone editing a pinned ledger
  will eventually be wrong about something.

## Not acceptable

- Posting a number as measured when it was copied, quoted, or recalled.
- Presenting a rounding of a pinned value as if it were the value.
- Silently editing a pinned number to make prose agree with it. Fix the prose
  or rebuild with a guard.
- Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private reporting on this
repository. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will acknowledge
the report, and will either act or explain why not.

Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions as decided by the maintainer.
